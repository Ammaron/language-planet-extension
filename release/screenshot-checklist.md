# AMO screenshot checklist — 0.2.0

Capture from the exact signed artifact submitted to AMO. Do not use desktop-responsive mockups as Android evidence.

- Firefox Android phone: fresh-install consent and full-tab onboarding.
- Firefox Android phone: device code and website approval disclosure.
- Firefox Android phone: highlighted article and contextual popup.
- Firefox Android phone: phrase/disambiguation result and audio control.
- Firefox Android phone: compact popup with status and site controls.
- Firefox Android tablet: portrait and landscape advanced settings.
- Firefox Desktop: connected popup and highlighted article.
- Sensitive-page proof: extension inactive on a password/authentication page.

Before upload, verify that screenshots contain no real email addresses, access codes, tokens, private vocabulary identifiers, or unrelated personal tabs and notifications.
